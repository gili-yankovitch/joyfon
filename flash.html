Firmware · RP2040 · WebUSB

Flash your JoyFon.

The RP2040's ROM bootloader takes firmware over USB, and a Chromium browser can talk to it directly through WebUSB — no picotool, no drive to hunt for, no Python. Works in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS and Android. Not Firefox, not Safari, and nothing on an iPhone — those can still download the firmware below and copy it onto the RPI-RP2 drive by hand.

Flash firmware

Put the JoyFon in the bootloader — hold BOOTSEL on the Pico while plugging the USB-C cable in — then press Flash and choose RP2 Boot. The image below is the build published with this site; or point it at a .uf2 you built yourself.

    Functionality test

    After flashing, connect the JoyFon — to this computer, or to the phone it will live on — then press any button (or Connect controller). Each button you press lights up on the silhouette, a quick check of the wiring and the firmware. Works on desktop and mobile.
