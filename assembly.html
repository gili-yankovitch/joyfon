Assembly guide

From the USB Daughterboard to the final button check. Follow the photos in order, with the correct fasteners and a check at every stage.

Start assembling ↓

Before you begin

Parts on the table.

  • Screws: 2x M2x4, 3x M2x8, 2x M3x20
  • 2x M3x3 brass threaded inserts
  • Boards: Main Controller PCB; USB Daughterboard
  • 10 cm / 5 cm, 6-pin, 0.5 mm pitch forward FFC ribbon cable
  • Hinge itself (with USB holder); hinge system height extender; hinge left half; hinge right half; hinge USB board cover
  • D-PAD; shoulder button; A; Y; X; B; shoulder button; Start + Select button
  • 2x 10 mm-diameter silicon feet; back plate; main body

Identify the Main Controller PCB and the USB Daughterboard. The USB Daughterboard carries the USB-C male plug and an FFC ribbon-cable connector; the two boards connect through the ribbon cable.

Prepare the hinge itself (the hinge with USB holder part), hinge system height extender, hinge left half, hinge right half, hinge USB board cover, main body and back plate.

Lay out the D-PAD, two identical shoulder buttons, A, Y, X and B buttons, and the symmetric Start + Select button piece. Prepare two 10 mm-diameter silicon feet.

Use a matching hex driver for the M2 screws and a Phillips screwdriver for the M3x20 screws. Have a soldering iron ready for the two M3x3 brass threaded inserts. Keep the phone disconnected during assembly.

Step 01 / 12

Connect the ribbon to the USB Daughterboard

Insert the 6-pin, 0.5 mm-pitch forward FFC ribbon into the connector on the USB Daughterboard, matching the orientation in the close-up. Seat it squarely.

Fit the board with its USB-C male plug aligned to its opening in the hinge with USB holder part. Fold the ribbon smoothly back and to the right to allow it to sit gently on the hinge's floor.

CHECK

The ribbon is seated squarely, folded smoothly back and to the right, and rests on the hinge's floor without twisting.

Step 02 / 12

Fit the hinge USB board cover

Fit the hinge USB board cover while keeping the ribbon clear of the mating surfaces. Check both seating points shown in the photos.

CHECK

The USB Daughterboard and hinge USB board cover are seated, the USB-C male plug is aligned, and the ribbon is not trapped.

Back to top ↑

Step 03 / 12

Route the ribbon and assemble the hinge system

Roll the cable excess twice and fit the rolled cable inside the open hollow of the hinge itself (the hinge with USB holder part).

Then, route the ribbon cable through the back opening of the hinge. Bring the separate hinge right half into position, as shown in the second photo. Slide the ribbon cable through the hinge right half slit on its circular cavity.

Slide the ribbon cable through the slit on the right side of the hinge left half's flat part. Slide the hinge itself into the left round cavity of the left hinge half, bringing the two hinge halves together.

Use the hinge system height extender and slide its pin down the hollow of the hinge halves. Align the ribbon with the right-side slit while bringing the hinge and the two halves together.

CHECK

Check that the cable is not pinched by the hinge itself or the hinge housings. Keep the ribbon aligned with the right-side slit as the parts come together.

Back to top ↑

Step 04 / 12

Prepare the two M3x20 hinge-system screws

Use the two M3x20 Phillips screws at the hinge-system mounting points shown.

Do not fully insert or tighten them yet. Advance each screw only until its far end is flush with the hinge top. Final tightening happens after the threaded inserts are installed in the main controller body.

CHECK

Both M3x20 screw tips are flush with the hinge top. Neither screw is fully tightened yet.

Back to top ↑

Step 05 / 12

Secure the Main Controller PCB to the back plate

Align the Main Controller PCB with the back plate and its mounting holes.

Use the two M2x4 hex-socket screws to hold the main PCB to the back plate. Fit the first screw, then the second, while keeping the PCB seated at both mounting points.

CHECK

Both M2x4 hex-socket screws are fitted and the PCB lies flat against its mounting points.

Back to top ↑

Step 06 / 12

Install the two M3x3 brass threaded inserts

Identify the two M3x3 brass threaded inserts and the two holes in the bottom of the controller main body. The two holes are indicated by arrows in the second photo.

Place the inserts upright in the holes. Use the soldering iron to warm and gently seat the inserts, then allow the body to cool.

CHECK

Ensure the threaded inserts are flush with the bottom plane of the controller main body and aligned on the horizontal axis.

Back to top ↑

Step 07 / 12

Attach the hinge system to the main controller body

Insert the ribbon cable through the right-side slit in the controller main body. Bring the hinge system into position while keeping the ribbon clear of the mating surfaces.

Seat the hinge system against the main controller body. Tighten the M3x20 screws holding the hinge system: they should align with and tighten into the respective M3x3 threaded inserts embedded in the controller body.

CHECK

Both M3x20 screws engage their respective inserts. The hinge system is seated and the ribbon is not pinched.

Back to top ↑

Step 08 / 12

Connect the ribbon to the Main Controller PCB

Turn the free ribbon end toward the Main Controller PCB connector, matching the fold and contact orientation shown.

Insert and secure the ribbon squarely. Keep enough relaxed cable for the Main Controller PCB and back plate to fold onto the main controller body without stretching or pinching the ribbon.

CHECK

The FFC cable connects the USB Daughterboard to the Main Controller PCB and has room to fold inside the body.

Back to top ↑

Step 09 / 12

Fit the D-PAD, shoulder buttons and face buttons

Work from the inside perspective shown. The D-PAD goes inside the round cavity to the right; notice and match the notch that orients it.

The two shoulder buttons are identical. Fit one into each upper corner. The Start + Select button piece is symmetric and goes at the bottom.

Arrange the ABXY buttons clockwise from the inside perspective: X, Y, B, A.

CHECK

The D-PAD notch is aligned, both shoulder buttons are seated, Start + Select is at the bottom, and the inside-view clockwise sequence is X-Y-B-A.

Back to top ↑

Step 10 / 12

Fit the back plate and fasten three M2x8 screws

Fold the Main Controller PCB and back plate onto the main controller body while guiding the ribbon into the internal space.

It might be easier to fit the bottom part of the back plate first, then slide in the top.

Use the three M2x8 hex-socket screws to close the back plate to the main controller body. Keep the panel seated while fastening all three mounting points.

CHECK

The back plate is seated evenly, all three M2x8 hex-socket screws are fitted, and the ribbon is clear of the seam.

Back to top ↑

Step 11 / 12

Stick the silicon feet to the outside of the back plate

Stick the two 10 mm-diameter silicon feet to the back panel's outside at the circular positions shown.

Press each silicon foot down so it adheres fully and its edge is seated.

CHECK

Both silicon feet stick to the outside of the back plate and remain clear of the seam.

Back to top ↑

Step 12 / 12

Connect the phone, then perform the final click-test

Align the USB-C male plug on the USB Daughterboard with the phone's USB socket. Support both parts and slide the plug straight into the socket until seated.

Check the completed connection, then perform the final click-test shown in the last photo.

CHECK

Check that all buttons click well and bounce back to the default position after the click. Check three times.
